## Description

Employer: Amazon.com Services LLC

Position: Financial Analyst III - AMZ21725.3

Location: New York, NY

Multiple Positions Available:

1\. Create and develop complex tax models and concisely summarize results to Tax and/or Finance leadership.

2\. Work across all tax groups, including M&A, Tax Planning, Tax Policy, Reporting and Compliance, and Tax Provision.

3\. Research the state, US federal and non-US tax implications of corporate tax projects, including but not limited to acquisitions and restructurings.

4\. Develop strong working relationships among cross-functional teams, including Accounting, Financial Planning and Analysis, and Tax Reporting.

5\. Work to understand overall operational, data, and information requirements for tax planning, including analyzing tax data/calculations/reports, and understanding Amazon's technology environment and interfaces.

(40 hours / week, 8:00am-5:00pm, Salary Range $146058 - $179500)

Amazon.com is an Equal Opportunity – Affirmative Action Employer – Minority / Female / Disability / Veteran / Gender Identity / Sexual Orientation

## Basic Qualifications

A Master’s degree or foreign equivalent in Tax, Finance or related field and 5 year(s) of experience in the job offered or a related occupation.

Must have 5 year(s) of experience in the following:

1\. Experience in tax, finance or a related analytical field.

2\. Experience in working in a large public accounting firm or multi-national corporate tax department.

3\. Experience in Microsoft Office products and applications at an advanced level.

4\. Experience in working at a large public accounting firm, law firm or multi-national corporate tax department

The base pay range for this position in New York City is USD $146058 - $179500 (yr). Pay is based on market location and may vary depending on job-related knowledge, skills, and experience. A sign-on payment and restricted stock units may be provided as part of the compensation package, in addition to a full range of medical, financial, or other benefits, dependent on the position offered. Applicants should apply via Amazon's internal or external careers site.
